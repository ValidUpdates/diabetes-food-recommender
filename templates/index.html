A two-stage decision support system for diabetes screening and Nigerian dietary guidance.

This system pairs a CART / random forest diabetes detection stage with a conditionally-invoked food recommendation stage, ranking suitable Nigerian foods by glycaemic index, fibre, and protein content for patients who screen positive.

The three interfaces below are components of Layer 1, the Application Layer. Layers 2 through 5 — preprocessing, detection, recommendation, and data — operate behind them and are not accessed directly.
